Under the hood

Header “STG2” + flags + length + salt · AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2-SHA-256 · 1–3 least significant bits per R, G, B channel · password-seeded shuffle chooses which channels carry the bits · lossless PNG or BMP only · images over 1600 px are resized for speed.

    🔒 Your images never leave your device. Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or seen by us.

    📄Text or any file

    Hide messages, PDFs, images or archives. The filename comes back too.

    🔑AES-256 on your device

    Your password never leaves the browser. Keys come from PBKDF2-SHA-256.

    🎲Random bit spreading
